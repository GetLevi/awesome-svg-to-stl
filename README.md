### What is SVG to STL Conversion?

SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) files are 2D images that use mathematical paths instead of pixels. STL (STereoLithography) files are the standard format for 3D printers. Converting SVG to STL lets you take any logo, design, or drawing and turn it into a physical object you can print.

## 💡 Tips for Successful Conversion

### Preparing Your SVG Files

- **Keep it simple** - Complex designs with thousands of paths can be difficult to convert. Start with basic shapes.
- **Use solid fills** - Ensure your SVG has proper fill colors. Outline-only designs may not convert well.
- **Check dimensions** - Make sure your SVG has a reasonable size. Very large or tiny dimensions can cause issues.
- **Close your paths** - Open paths may not convert correctly. Make sure all shapes are closed.

### Optimizing for 3D Printing

- **Add a base** - Most converters offer a "base plate" option. This gives your model stability.
- **Adjust extrusion height** - Taller extrusions create more detailed models but take longer to print.
- **Consider wall thickness** - Thin elements might break during printing. Increase thickness where needed.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### "My conversion fails"

- Ensure your SVG file isn't corrupted
- Try simplifying complex designs
- Check that your SVG uses standard paths and shapes

### "The 3D model looks wrong"

- Adjust the extrusion height
- Check your base plate settings
- Verify your SVG has no overlapping paths

### "The STL won't print properly"

- Make sure your model has proper wall thickness
- Check for non-manifold edges
- Use the preview tools to inspect your model
